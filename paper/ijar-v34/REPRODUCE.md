Check out tag `v0.6.4` and run
`python paper/ijar-v34/verify_release.py`. The verifier reproduces the
manuscript-to-artifact map and count/evidence-level claims. The public release
does not claim end-to-end model-training reproduction.

The Figure 2 validity denominator is 443 proposal-feasible conditions. Seven
proposal-infeasible reject-all placeholders remain only in the 450-condition
utility roster. The valid clientwise Bonferroni reference protects the same
worst-client target. The pooled fixed-quota curve does not and is retained only
as a mechanism diagnostic, not a method ranking.

At the primary setting, H, S, and B share the target, candidate family,
confidence budget, and realized count tensor. S-minus-B isolates conservative
client-count division within the simple family. H-minus-S changes the family
decision. Fixed empirical reservoirs, registered finite frames, descriptive
bootstrap intervals, retrospective planning calculations, and source-disjoint
reuse of frozen checkpoints do not establish population transport or
prospective independent confirmation.
