# Reproduction boundary

Check out tag `v0.6.3` and run `python paper/ijar-v33/verify_release.py`.
The release verifies the manuscript-to-artifact map and the count/evidence-level
certification boundary. End-to-end model training is not claimed. Licensed or
large source data, checkpoints, and record-level logits are not included.

The Figure 2 validity denominator is 443 proposal-feasible conditions; seven
proposal-infeasible reject-all placeholders remain only in the 450-condition
utility roster. The fixed-size empirical-reservoir results target the frozen
reservoir, not an unobserved deployment population. Bootstrap intervals are
descriptive, audit-frontier multipliers are retrospective planning diagnostics,
and finite-frame results target only their registered frames. Source-disjoint
evaluation images do not constitute independent retraining or prospective
confirmation.
